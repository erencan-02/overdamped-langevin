    Using the app

    Brush mode

    • Hold left mouse to raise Ψ ( warm)
    • Hold right mouse to lower Ψ ( cool)
    • The force field toggle draws −∇Ψ as arrows
    • The brush is a Gaussian: the circles show 1σ and 2σ

    Particle mode

    • Click to drop a particle
    • Shift + drag to spray many
    • Right-drag a rectangle to fill it with a grid of particles (spacing in the panel)
    • Right click a particle to remove it

    Potential Ψ

    • Painted (default): brush, presets, images and the camera, on a lattice with |Ψ| ≤ 5
    • Formula (Potential window): type Ψ(t, x, y) in LaTeX, e.g. \frac{x^2+y^2}{2}; the gradient is derived exactly, with no lattice and no cap
    • Ψ may use x, y, r, θ, time t and σ; click an example to try it

    Birth / death

    • Every particle has two exponential clocks: births at rate λ, death at rate μ
    • A birth adds a particle at the parent's position (same colour), a death removes it
    • The plot compares N(t) with its expectation N₀e(λ−μ)t; recordings store every birth and death

    3D view

    • 2D / 3D (top, or 2 / 3): Ψ rises into a relief, particles sit at height Ψ
    • The brush and the particle tools work on the surface as in 2D (except the right-drag grid)
    • Middle-drag or Alt + drag to orbit, Shift + middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom; one finger orbits on touch screens
    • The live camera works here too: the camera image rises into a moving relief
    • Drop images, recordings and scenarios here too: the view moves to where they are placed

    Images

    • Drop an image on the left half to turn it into Ψ: dark areas become valleys
    • Drop it on the right half to turn it into particles, densest where it is darkest
    • Light drawing on dark? Use Invert afterwards
    • The camera button turns your front camera into a live Ψ

    Analysis & recording

    • Observable (left): type a test function f(x, y) in LaTeX and compare the particle mean of f (with its 95 % band) to the exact ∫ f dπ
    • KL to equilibrium (left): live plot, kernel variance, box
    • Show ρ^h draws the smoothed particle density in pink
    • Record button: start / stop a run; downloads as .npz in the Recordings panel (right)
    • Drop an .npz to replay it: the player at the bottom scrubs through the run; changing Ψ, particles or a physics setting leaves playback and continues from there
    • Seed (panel): the same seed and the same actions give exactly the same run
